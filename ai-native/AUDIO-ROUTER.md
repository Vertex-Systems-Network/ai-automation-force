# Audio Production Router

## Purpose

Do not send every content type to the same audio model. Route approved content to the audio system that matches the intended final artifact.

The AI operator owns the audio direction. The human should not need to specify genre, BPM, instruments, background music, vocal tone, or arrangement for every item. The system must infer those choices from the approved content, age band, emotional objective, scene structure, language, and safety policy, then record the decisions in the content package.

## Core rule

- Speech is generated as speech.
- Music is generated as music.
- Narration with background music is a two-stem production: narration stem + instrumental music bed, then deterministic mixing.
- A full song is generated by a music model capable of vocals + arrangement.

Never ask a TTS model to act as a full music-production engine.

## Route A — Speech / narration

Use for:
- story
- bedtime-story
- poem when spoken
- rhyme when spoken
- educational-narration
- guided-imagination
- spoken lullaby

Preferred provider family: Gemini TTS.

Preferred model at foundation time:
- `gemini-3.1-flash-tts-preview`

Free-tier fallback preference:
- `gemini-2.5-flash-preview-tts` when the current free tier supports it.

Paid-quality fallback:
- `gemini-2.5-pro-preview-tts`

Before execution, verify current model IDs, free-tier availability, quotas and terms because preview offerings can change.

### Autonomous narration direction

Before compiling the TTS prompt, the AI must infer and persist:
- narrator age presentation;
- female-presenting voice requirement;
- warmth/energy;
- speaking pace;
- pause behavior;
- emotional arc;
- articulation level;
- pronunciation notes;
- whether the piece needs a separate background music bed.

Default voice direction:
- adult female narrator;
- warm, natural and reassuring;
- clear child-friendly articulation;
- expressive without distracting theatricality;
- no real-person or celebrity imitation;
- pace adapted to the age band.

### TTS prompt structure

1. Audio Profile
2. Scene / listener context
3. Director's Notes
4. Exact frozen script
5. Pronunciation Notes
6. Prohibitions

## Route B — Full musical audio

Use for:
- song
- sung lullaby
- musical rhyme
- musical learning track

Preferred provider family: Lyria through Gemini API.

Preferred full-track model:
- `lyria-3-pro-preview`

Short concept model:
- `lyria-3-clip-preview`

Google's current Lyria guidance says the model can infer expected instrumentation from a genre, but more specific prompts provide more control. Therefore the AI music director should decide how prescriptive to be rather than forcing the human to fill every music field.

### Autonomous Music Director

The AI must analyze the approved content and autonomously decide:
- genre / production family;
- BPM or tempo range;
- key / scale when useful;
- instrumentation or whether to allow genre-default instrumentation;
- percussion density;
- bass behavior;
- harmonic texture;
- singer profile;
- intro / verse / chorus / bridge / outro structure;
- energy curve;
- repetition and call-response strategy;
- duration target;
- ending behavior for video editing;
- mix priorities;
- age-appropriate sound-design limits.

The final prompt must contain the decisions. It must not leave unresolved placeholders for the operator unless a genuinely human creative decision is required.

### Default female singer direction

- adult female voice;
- warm, friendly, clean timbre;
- clear consonants and vowels;
- comfortable non-strained range;
- child-friendly energy;
- no seductive, aggressive, frightening, overly mature or celebrity-like styling;
- no imitation of a known singer.

### Lullaby direction

- soft female vocal;
- gentle tempo;
- low dynamic range;
- sparse arrangement;
- smooth transitions;
- no abrupt percussion or startling transients;
- avoid overstimulation.

## Route C — Rhythmic spoken chant

Use Gemini TTS with explicit rhythmic cadence instructions when a rhyme/learning piece should remain spoken rather than sung.

If backing music is needed, use Route D rather than pretending the TTS output contains a proper instrumental arrangement.

## Route D — Narration + background music

Use for stories, poems, guided imagination and educational narration when music improves the experience.

Production is two independent stems:

1. `voice.wav`
   - generated by Gemini TTS or current selected speech provider;
   - exact approved script;
   - female narrator by default.

2. `music-bed.wav`
   - generated by Lyria or another approved music provider;
   - instrumental by default;
   - no competing lead vocal;
   - arrangement automatically derived from the story arc and age band.

Then mix deterministically:
- preserve speech intelligibility above all else;
- duck music under important dialogue;
- fade music at scene boundaries where useful;
- no sudden level jumps;
- no startling effects for younger audiences;
- final peak/loudness checks before approval.

### Autonomous background-music plan

For every narrated piece that uses a music bed, the AI must generate a timeline-aware plan such as:
- opening mood;
- primary instruments / texture;
- tempo range;
- emotional changes by story section;
- where music should thin out or pause;
- ending resolution;
- prohibited sounds;
- target bed level beneath narration.

The music model may infer ordinary genre instrumentation when that improves simplicity, but any narratively important instrument or musical event must be explicitly prompted.

## Routing decision fields

Every content package must include:
- `audio_mode`: `speech` | `music` | `chant` | `speech_with_bed`
- `audio_provider_family`
- `preferred_model`
- `female_voice_required`: true by default
- `music_direction_mode`: `autonomous`
- `music_bed_required`: boolean
- `voice_prompt_path`
- `music_prompt_path` when applicable
- `mix_plan_path` when applicable

## Free-tier policy

Free-tier capability is preferred when it satisfies:
- API or explicitly permitted automation access;
- current quota availability;
- required quality/capability;
- acceptable licensing for the intended use;
- acceptable watermark/privacy/data-use terms.

A free consumer UI is not automatically an automatable API. Never automate a website in a way that violates provider terms, and never create/rotate accounts to bypass quotas.

## Capability honesty

Never claim that TTS produced a full musical arrangement. Never claim exact vocal identity, exact melody, or cross-model continuity when a provider does not guarantee it. Record the actual provider/model, prompt, run ID, quota class, license class and output fingerprint for every render.
